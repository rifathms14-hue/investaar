# Round 2 concepts

Four mark directions from the nuqta, bilingual monogram, pen-cut dot and square Kufic ideas, with a touch of the references you shared. Each folder has horizontal, stacked and mark SVGs in colour, reversed, black and white. Lockups use the same phi spacing as the other options. Text is outlined.

| Folder | Idea |
| --- | --- |
| `nuqta/` | Five pen-cut dots. Four parties around one point, and the point is Emerald |
| `block-r-reh/` | A solid R with the Arabic reh cut into its bowl |
| `penring/` | Four arcs split by cuts at a pen angle, around one dot |
| `kufic/` | A square with three right-angled contour cuts, so four bands |

`concepts.py` holds the geometry. `concepts-board.png` is the overview.

These are first constructions. Nothing here is snapped to the golden ladder yet. That comes after you pick.

## Twin r (added after feedback)

`twin-r/` and `twin-r-board.png`. The Latin r and the Arabic reh are the same letter turned 180 degrees. One module with pen-cut ends is used twice, dark for r and green for ر. The first four concepts above were judged weak and are kept only for the record.
