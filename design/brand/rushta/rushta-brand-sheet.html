Concept

Four papers.
Four parties.
One point.

Rushta is a prescription workflow that connects doctors, patients, the pharmacy and the clinic. The mark is four sheets of paper coming together to form a medical cross, so all four are connected at a single point.

CarefulPlainExactLocalAccountable

The idea

Four papers, one cross

The story behind the mark.

  1. Four papersEach arm is a sheet: a prescription, a record, a document.
  2. Four partiesOne sheet for each: doctors, patients, the pharmacy and the clinic.
  3. One pointThe sheets come together at the centre, so all four are connected at a single point.
  4. Soft corners, sharp tipsCorners soften like the edge of a page. The tips are where each sheet folds in.

روشتة is the Arabic word for a prescription. The lockup sets it first, in Arabic, and gives the English equal width.

Construction

One unit, u

The unit is the mark height divided by eight. Mark to text is 2u. The text block is 8.5u wide. Clear space is 2u on every side.

Care

What not to do

Six of twelve misuse rules. The full set is in the guidelines.

Don't stretch or squash.

Don't recolour the mark.

Don't retype the name in another font.

Don't add shadows or effects.

Don't mirror it for right-to-left layouts.

Don't use the colour version on Emerald.
