# Rushta option R: the folded page

A second mark direction. One sheet of paper, folded at the corner, forms the letter R. The medical cross is cut out of the page. The pinwheel in the main folder stays as it is.

Open `construction/` for the drawings. Every SVG here has outlined text and needs no installed font.

## How the mark is built

One unit, `u`, which is the mark height divided by phi to the fifth power. At a 451 px mark, `u` is 40.7 px. Every size is a step of phi in `u`.

| Part | Rule | In u |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Width | 12u | 12 |
| Height | phi to the fifth | 11.09 |
| Fold column | width divided by phi | 7.42 |
| Head | A golden rectangle, width by width divided by phi. The bowl underside sits on its bottom edge | 12 by 7.42 |
| Page corners, fold notch, curl top | phi times u | 1.618 |
| Swoosh around the bowl | phi squared times u | 2.618 |
| Fold corner, bowl top | u | 1 |
| Bowl underside | What is left of the golden rectangle after the fold line | 2.09 |
| Cross | 2u clear on each side of the page column. phi squared times u clear above. Centred on the fold line. Arm thickness is arm length divided by phi squared | 3.42 long, 1.31 thick |
| Leg | Left edge at 2 phi u, right edge at 2 phi cubed u, run 3u | |

The circles are in `construction/rushta-r-construction-circles`. The spiral drawing shows that the first cut of the golden rectangle lands on the fold line, and the spiral hugs the bowl.

Reversed puts the page in white. Black and white versions are one colour with a thin seam, `u` divided by phi to the fourth, so the fold and the leg still read. The seam is 2.6 px at a 200 px mark and disappears below about 40 px. Use the mark at 24 px in colour only.

## The lockup

Same system as the pinwheel. Arabic over English, black dots, Madani Arabic Medium and Satoshi Bold, both lines justified to one width. The text block is as wide as the mark, which is 216 px when the mark is 200 px tall. Mark to text is 61.8 px horizontal, 38.2 px stacked. The Arabic descender sits 23.6 px above the English cap top. Clear space is 61.8 px.

## Fit to the reference

The golden version overlaps the reference by 97 percent on the page piece. Three things moved on purpose. The bowl underside went up 6 px of 451 to land on the golden rectangle. The cross moved 3.7 px right of where it was drawn, to sit in the middle of the page column. The leg top followed the bowl.

## Honest comparison with the pinwheel

The R reads as a letter in half a second and carries the name. That is its win. It also gives up the story. The pinwheel is four sheets meeting at one point, which is doctors, patients, the pharmacy and the clinic. The R is one sheet. If the four-persona idea is the brand, the R needs a different job, such as an app icon, with the pinwheel kept as the brand mark.

The R is also close to other folded-page medical marks. A trademark search should come before a decision.
